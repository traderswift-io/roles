# Market reviewer

## What it is

TraderSwift builds tools for people who run trading bots and desks: Signer for signing agent orders, Signals, Charts and a hub chain. We know what we've built. We need someone who tests who will pay for it, and why.

## What you own

- **Two or three real conversations a month** with people who run trading bots or desks. You find out what they use now, what hurts, and what they'd pay to fix.
- **A review of pricing.** Does it match what you heard?
- **A review of the first-user plan.** Who are the first users, how do we reach them, and is that realistic?

## What you don't

Code, trading, money, keys, hiring. Product decisions stay with the founder. You don't sell; you listen and report.

## Your week

About 4 hours.

- **1 hour doing:** one conversation, or the notes from one.
- **3 hours thinking:** read the pricing and the plan, compare them with what people told you, and write down where they don't match.

## Who it suits

Someone who has run or sold to trading desks, bot operators or quant teams. You know the difference between "that's interesting" and "I'd pay for that". You're happy to tell a founder the market doesn't want something.

## How to say you're interested

Reply to whoever sent you this, or open an issue in this repo.
