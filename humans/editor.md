# Editor: the brief and the cut

## What it is

TraderSwift is trading infrastructure built by one founder and a team of AI agents. The agents write fast. What they need is someone who decides what a piece must say before it's written, and checks every claim before it goes out.

That's this role.

## What you own

- **The brief.** Before anything public is written, you set what it must answer and how long it can be. That covers blog posts, the guide, READMEs, launch notes and pitches.
- **The cut.** Before it's published, you check each claim against the record. If the record doesn't show it, it comes out.
- **A monthly keep/park list.** What gets published or launched this month, measured against the plan. What waits.

## What you don't

Code, trading, money, keys, hiring. Product decisions stay with the founder.

You're not a gate. Work doesn't wait for you; if a piece ships before your cut, your cut lands as a correction in the next version.

## Your week

About 4 hours.

- **1 hour doing:** write or approve one or two briefs, and mark up one draft with cuts.
- **3 hours thinking:** read what went out and what's queued, check it against the record, and judge what's worth saying at all.

## Who it suits

Someone who has edited or commissioned technical writing for traders or developers. You're comfortable saying "this isn't true yet" and "this is too long". You'd rather publish less and be right.
